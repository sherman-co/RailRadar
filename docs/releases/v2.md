## RailRadar v2 — more accurate on Raja and MrBilit

### Download
Download **RailRadar-windows.zip**, extract it over your previous folder, and run `RailRadar.exe`. Your settings and searches are kept.

### Improvements
- **Raja prices are read correctly.** Raja writes the currency with Arabic letters (ريال); these are now normalized, so prices, "sold out" and remaining capacity are detected.
- **MrBilit sold-out trains are counted.** MrBilit shows full trains with no price and no text, only a hidden "not reservable" marker. These are now recognized as full.
- **No more merged cards.** When one card on a page was not recognized, it could merge with the next one. Each train is now always read on its own.
- **Temporary site errors are shown.** Messages such as Utravs' "an error occurred, try again in a few minutes" now appear as a notice instead of a silent "0 trains".

### Features
Everything from v1: live panel, any ticket site, parallel checks, minimum-seat alerts, trains-only filter, Bale and Telegram control, data saving, and outage detection.
